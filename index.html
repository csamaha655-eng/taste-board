Principles

Six rulesbehind every screen.

Pulled from the references you sent and everything you've rejected before.

  • Rich, never busy

    Every screen is full of real, useful content. Nothing empty, nothing there just to decorate.

  • Solid color only

    No gradients on anything. Depth comes from light, glass and soft shadow.

  • One accent, at most

    Ink, a quiet gray and hairlines do the work. The main action is a black pill.

  • Glass is for navigation

    Bars, floating controls and sheets are glass. Content stays solid and easy to read.

  • Bold, never skinny

    One typeface per product. Headings at 500 to 700 with tight tracking.

  • Calm motion

    Short, physical easing. No splash animations, no confetti, no scroll-jacking.

Easing

cubic-bezier(.22, 1, .36, 1) on the web, .smooth and .snappy springs on iOS.

Duration

160 ms for hover, 320 ms for interface changes, 560 ms for reveals. One orchestrated moment per page, at most.

Respect

Reduce Motion makes everything static. Reduce Transparency turns glass solid. Nothing waits invisible for a script.

How it works

Ask for an app.The taste comes with it.

  1. 01

    Read the project

    An existing app keeps its own look. Your newest words beat everything.

  2. 02

    Pick a theme for the subject

    Travel gets parallax, a planner gets frosted sheets, a website gets Quiet Glass.

  3. 03

    Build in phases

    Screen by screen, never one shot.

  4. 04

    Screenshot and check

    Full size, light and dark, phone width, mid-scroll, against the Never list.

  5. 05

    Show it short

    Before and after, two or three lines, and what to test on your phone.
